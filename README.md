## 🕹️ About

Castlevania: Belmont's Curse is a 2D action-exploration game developed by Evil Empire and Motion Twin with KONAMI. Set in 1499 Paris, it follows Rose Belmont as she battles through gothic streets, catacombs, and Dracula's castle using the legendary Vampire Killer. Join the pre-release beta test, help shape the experience, and receive a free game key.

Designed for players who love exploration-driven 2D action, gothic fantasy, challenging combat, and classic Castlevania atmosphere.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Explore Gothic Paris** — Freely explore richly crafted maps across medieval Paris and Dracula's castle.
- ⚔️ **Whip-Based Combat** — Master fast, fluid movement and the iconic Vampire Killer whip.
- 🃏 **Arcana System** — Seal defeated bosses into tarot cards and use their skills in combat.
- 👹 **Legendary Bosses** — Face classic foes including Death, Medusa, and Carmilla alongside new threats.
- 🧰 **Customize Your Style** — Combine weapons and Relics to create a battle style that fits your approach.
- 🦇 **Castlevania Atmosphere** — Experience vivid visuals, gothic environments, and a fresh take on the series.
- 🧪 **Help Shape the Beta** — Share feedback, discover issues, and help refine the adventure before launch.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**A:** Simply sign in with your Steam account, browse available beta programs, request access to games you're interested in, and start testing. After you complete the beta, you'll receive a free Steam key for the full game upon its official release.

**Q: Do I need to pay to join?**
**A:** No, joining a beta test is completely free. All we ask for is your time and genuine feedback.

**Q: Is my Steam account safe?**
**A:** Yes. We use Steam's official sign-in, so we never see or store your password. Your account stays fully under your control.

**Q: When will I receive the Steam key?**
**A:** Keys are sent via email once the game you tested officially releases on Steam. We'll notify you at the email address associated with your Steam account.

**Q: Will I get access right away?**
**A:** After you sign in with Steam, your tester profile needs to be approved. Approvals go out as spots free up — roughly once a week. Once approved, beta tests become visible and requestable.

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
